Global Black Unity Movement

Unity Without Uniformity. Progress Through Cooperation.

GBUM aims to connect Black communities in Haiti, Africa, the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, and the wider diaspora through political accountability, economic cooperation, and community development.

Express volunteer interest

Mission

To connect Black communities across borders so that they can hold power to account, cooperate economically, and build up their own communities, while respecting that each community has its own history, culture, and priorities.

Vision

A world where Black communities are connected by shared purpose and mutual respect, and where cooperation, not uniformity, drives progress.

Three pillars

Political accountability

Encouraging transparency, civic participation, and responsible leadership in the places where we work.

Economic cooperation

Exploring ways for Black communities and businesses to share knowledge, trade, and opportunity across regions.

Community development

Supporting education and practical community needs, led by the people who live them.

Haiti first

GBUM intends to start in Haiti, beginning with education and practical community support, and to learn from that work before growing to other regions. No programs, results, or numbers are claimed here, because the movement is at the beginning.

Principles

  • Unity without uniformity: we cooperate across differences.
  • Progress through cooperation, not rivalry.
  • Honesty: we do not claim what has not happened.
  • Community-led: local voices lead local work.
  • Respect for every language, including Haitian Creole, French, and English.
